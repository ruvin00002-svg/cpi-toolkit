CPI Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026. Publisher: the CPI Toolkit project, https://github.com/ruvin00002-svg/cpi-toolkit. Contact for privacy questions: ruvink44@gmail.com, or an issue in the repository.

CPI Toolkit is a browser extension for SAP Cloud Integration. This policy describes what the extension does with data. It applies to version 5.0.1 and later until it is updated.

Summary

  • The extension has no server. The publisher does not receive, collect, sell or share any data from it.
  • It reads data from your own SAP tenant with the session you already have in the browser, and keeps what it needs in your browser.
  • It sends data to an AI service only if you configure one, and only after you approve each request.
  • It sends requests to other addresses only when you enter them (API Test Studio, SAP API host with your service key), after your browser asks for permission for that address.

Data the extension handles

Data Where it comes from What happens with it
Content of SAP Cloud Integration pages and SAP APIs (integration flows, message logs, error texts, configuration) Your tenant, read with your browser session Shown and analysed in your browser. Message logs and error texts read for analysis stay in memory.
Settings, history, review results, snapshots, projects, approved documents, test data, audit log You and the tenant Stored in the browser's extension storage on your computer (chrome.storage.local); never synced. Listed with sizes and deletable in Release > Governance.
AI service key You By default kept only until the browser closes (chrome.storage.session). If you choose "On this computer", it is stored encrypted and expires after 30 days.
API Test Studio credentials You Secret values kept until the browser closes, unless you choose to remember them on this computer.
Text sent to an AI service Your question and the tool results the AI asks for Sent only to the AI address you configured, only after you approve the exact request. Passwords, tokens and keys are removed before sending; tenant names and parameter values are replaced unless you allow them. A log of requests (details and a fingerprint by default) stays in your browser.
Usage counters Your use of the extension Counts of features used and AI tokens, kept in your browser only, never sent. Your organization can switch them off.

Third parties

  • SAP: the extension talks to your SAP tenant as you would in the browser. SAP's terms and privacy policy apply to your tenant.
  • Your AI provider (only if you configure one): what you approve is sent to that provider; its terms and privacy policy apply. The extension does not include any AI service.
  • Addresses you enter in the API Test Studio or as an SAP API host receive the requests you send to them.

No data is used for advertising, sold, used to decide creditworthiness or lending, or used for any purpose other than the features you use.

Your control

  • Switch the AI assistant off, or never configure it.
  • Delete stored data in Release > Governance (by category, with a confirmation), or remove the extension, which removes its storage.
  • Your organization may set rules through browser policy (for example approved AI addresses, retention periods).

Security

The extension follows the browser's Manifest V3 rules: no code is loaded from the internet, extension pages run with a strict content security policy, and actions that change a tenant are off by default.
