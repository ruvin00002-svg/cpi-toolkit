CPI Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: the day version 5.1.0 is published (the policy of 20 November 2026 applies to 5.0.1). Publisher: the CPI Toolkit project, https://github.com/ruvin00002-svg/cpi-toolkit. Contact for privacy questions: ruvink44@gmail.com, or an issue in the repository.

CPI Toolkit is a browser extension for SAP Cloud Integration. This policy describes what the extension does with data. It applies to version 5.1.0 and later until it is updated.

Summary

  • It reads data from your own SAP tenant with the session you already have in the browser, and keeps what it needs in your browser.
  • The built-in AI (Claude by Anthropic) works without setting anything up. What you approve goes through the CPI Toolkit AI relay, a small server run by the publisher, to Anthropic. The relay does not keep the text of your questions or the answers. It keeps a random CPI Toolkit ID of your install and, per day, how many questions and requests it sent, their size in tokens and their cost, to apply the daily limits and pay for the service.
  • You can switch the AI off, or use your own AI service instead (AI settings, Advanced). Then nothing goes to the relay.
  • Apart from the built-in AI, the publisher does not receive any data, and it never sells or shares data.
  • The extension sends requests to other addresses only when you enter them (API Test Studio, SAP API host with your service key), after your browser asks for permission for that address.

Data the extension handles

Data Where it comes from What happens with it
Content of SAP Cloud Integration pages and SAP APIs (integration flows, message logs, error texts, configuration) Your tenant, read with your browser session Shown and analysed in your browser. Message logs and error texts read for analysis stay in memory.
Settings, history, review results, snapshots, projects, approved documents, test data, audit log You and the tenant Stored in the browser's extension storage on your computer (chrome.storage.local); never synced. Listed with sizes and deletable in Release > Governance.
Text sent to the AI Your question, what is open on the page, the case notes and project you work on, and the tool results the AI asks for Sent only after you approve the exact request: with the built-in AI to the CPI Toolkit AI relay and from there to Anthropic; with your own AI service to that service. URLs, host names, e-mail and IP addresses, passwords, tokens and keys are removed before sending; tenant names and parameter values are replaced unless you allow them. Message payloads and the iFlow file are never sent. A log of requests (details and a fingerprint by default) stays in your browser.
CPI Toolkit ID Made at random by your install of the extension Sent with each built-in AI request so the relay can count your questions. It is not linked to your name or e-mail address. Shown in AI settings, so you can give it to the publisher (for a different limit, or to have its counts deleted).
Usage counts in the relay Your use of the built-in AI Per CPI Toolkit ID and day: number of questions and requests, tokens, cost. Kept 13 months, monthly totals 25 months, then deleted. The relay does not store IP addresses or the text of requests and answers.
AI service key (own AI service only) You By default kept only until the browser closes (chrome.storage.session). If you choose "On this computer", it is stored encrypted and expires after 30 days. The built-in AI needs no key.
API Test Studio credentials You Secret values kept until the browser closes, unless you choose to remember them on this computer.
Usage counters in the browser Your use of the extension Counts of features used and AI tokens, kept in your browser only, never sent. Your organization can switch them off.

Third parties

  • SAP: the extension talks to your SAP tenant as you would in the browser. SAP's terms and privacy policy apply to your tenant.
  • Anthropic (built-in AI): processes the AI requests you approve on the publisher's behalf, under Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal).
  • Cloudflare: hosts the CPI Toolkit AI relay; the requests pass through Cloudflare's network (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
  • Your own AI provider (only if you set one up): what you approve is sent to that provider; its terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Addresses you enter in the API Test Studio or as an SAP API host receive the requests you send to them.

No data is used for advertising, sold, used to decide creditworthiness or lending, or used for any purpose other than the features you use.

Your control

  • Switch the AI assistant off (AI settings: Use AI), or use your own AI service instead (AI settings, Advanced).
  • Every AI request is shown to you before it is sent; you can refuse it.
  • Ask the publisher to delete the counts of your CPI Toolkit ID (give the ID shown in AI settings).
  • Delete stored data in Release > Governance (by category, with a confirmation), or remove the extension, which removes its storage.
  • Your organization may set rules through browser policy (for example approved AI addresses, retention periods, or the AI switched off).

Security

The extension follows the browser's Manifest V3 rules: no code is loaded from the internet, extension pages run with a strict content security policy, and actions that change a tenant are off by default. The publisher's Anthropic key is kept only on the relay, never in the extension. The relay accepts only CPI Toolkit's own request shape and model.
